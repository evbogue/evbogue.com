I build tools for an independent internet: software for publishing, talking to people, and keeping the things we make.

These projects develop together. ANProto provides a small foundation for signed messages. Wiredove turns it into a social application. This website uses that application for its timeline. Alongside them, I maintain Secure Scuttlebot Classic, including a network that can carry the projects’ own source code.

## Where this work comes from

I have been publishing on the web since 1999. Before the protocols, there was the writing: personal sites, journalism, a stint at Gawker, and photo editing at New York Magazine. In 2009 I left corporate media to publish for myself.

Far Beyond the Stars and the ebooks taught me how to reach readers directly. Working on Decent in 2016, then Bogbook and ANProto, brought the same question into software: can you publish what you make, and reach the people who want it, without asking anyone for permission?

The projects keep changing because using them keeps revealing what needs work. This site is where the writing and the software meet.

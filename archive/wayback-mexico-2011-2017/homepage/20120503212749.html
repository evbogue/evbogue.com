About Ev

In 2009, I quit my job and started throwing out my stuff. I've been living out of a backpack since. I'm a location independent entrepreneur. I'm the author of Minimalist Business. I live and work from anywhere in the world.

I'm interested in how we communicate with humans and machines on the modern web. Technology is evolving at an exponential rate, we're evolving with it.

All of my Things pack down to a single backpack. This way, I can live flexibly in a changing world.

You are not a minimalist

September 04, 2014

I’m sitting here, on the floor, sipping a 1.2L of Victoria. They go fast, but at $28 pesos from the bodega down the way, it’s not much trouble to replen.

I’m sandwiched between two storms. The hurricane rolling in from the left, the tropical storm rolling to from the right. Deep down somewhere there is a sense I should run from this storm. But I stay put, because I know it’s wetter outside than it is inside. My Aztec king view is nothing but obscured.

And Tom Waits lurks in the background…

But what of minimalism? Minimalisticism. You are not a minimalist, if you were, you’d be freer than you are, now wouldn’t you be?

The question is, when people are looking at photos of Ukrainian refugees in tents in Russia, and their situation is more glamerous than their own situation in the “greatest country in the world” los Estados Unidos, what have we come to? What kind of world do we live in now? Should I be drinking twice as many 1.2Ls of Victoria as I am right now? Would that make it better.

I’d rather live in a “mud pit” than live in a delusion.

I’m going to take off September 9th through 11th. I’m just going to sit in solitude, play solitaire, and drink myself to sleep. No emails, no checking the Internet. Why? Because on the 9th is Apple releasing a “free” (with a two-year prison plan) iPhone that no one cares about (but people will pretend to care about) and 9/11 is the anniversary of… well, that is still a big dispute, now isn’t it?

No doubt the Internet will be so lit up by another terrible post-Steve Apple launch, that I won’t be able to get a word in. And 9/11 will be so lit up with US government propaganda that I won’t be able to get a word in. So instead I’ll just sit on the floor with a 1.2L of Victoria and pass the time. What else is there to do during days such as these?

Maybe I’ll get some much needed work in on the work I’ve been doing behind the scenes. It’ll be ready soon. But I doubt many of you are brave enough to ask about it.

If you lay down, and I piled all of your shit on top of you, and you tried to stand up, would you be able to? Would you die under the weight? Then you are not a minimalist. You’d be flat.

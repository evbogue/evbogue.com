Hi, I’m Ev




I’m a digital evolutionist. The digital world is constantly changing, this is why I write a free daily letter and blog about experiments in the digital world. I write a bi-weekly deeper, intimate, hands-on letter about evolving your digital work.

I’m curious about the ways we make money online by creating work in the evolving digital space. The way we publish is evolving rapidly — we’re still reading, but in very different places. The destiny of the digital word is uncertain, but I’m determined to find out what that future is. I experiment and report back on what’s working. If it’s not? I untether.

I believe in taking care of our bodies in the digital age. This is why I practice yoga almost daily (I’m a 200 hour certified yoga teacher), and I eat as well as I can (raw/vegetarian as much as possible.) I live with only a few Things.

Who reads me? Writers, publishers, creatives, artists, yogis, location independent workers, traveling adventurers, and those who make a living from publishing online.

My work has been featured on The CBS Evening News with Katie Couric, The Guardian, and The Huffington Post. I’ve spoken about digital publishing at Stanford University.

I live my life in coffee shops and tea houses throughout the world. Maybe we’ll chat over a cup of coffee or tea someday?

How has your experience of my work been? Drop me a line on Google+. evbogue@gmail.com or 347-274-9878.

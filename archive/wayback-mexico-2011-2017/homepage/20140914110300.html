What if The Singularity is the Holocaust?

September 14, 2014

Feeling: Incredulous
Ingesting: 1.2L of Victoria

There’s been a couple of important films in the vein of The Singularity is Near. You’ve probably watched a few them. Her. Transcendent Man. There’s another one with Johnny Depp I haven’t seen yet, but no one has recommended to me either.

Today I watched The Congress (2013), and I won’t spoil it for you. It’s a very Jewish movie. I’m not a Jew, but maybe I should be granted honary membership under the condition that my foreskin remains intact.

Anyway, if you’re not on Acid during The Congress, you’ll probably trip. And if you are on Acid, you will trip harder. I haven’t been impressed by many movies from the past two years, but this one did me in.

The question it brought up to me is this: what if The Singularity is a delusion?

I mean look around you.

SHUT THE FUCK UP AND LOOK UP FROM YOUR IPHONE AND LOOK AROUND YOU.

Okay. Do things look better to you than they did 20 years ago? Do they look better than 2010?

Okay then. I’d argue that The Singularity is not nearer. The only thing that’s happened is we’ve been deluded into thinking that centralized-authoritarian-cloud-based technologies should fuck us harder than they would have if we’d had the sense to not use them.

And if I’m looking at an exponential chart of the current rate of how fast we are being fucked by centralized technologies, I can only come to the conclusion that The Singularity is The Holocaust.

Look up from your iPhone/Fecesbook page. Are you marching towards that new home that awaits you on an off-world colony?

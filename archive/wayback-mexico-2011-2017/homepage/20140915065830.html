Uninspired

September 15, 2014

Feeling: Uninspired
Ingesting: Air

I’m feeling uninspired. Perhaps a bit tired. There’s been a storm blowing over La Ciudad all day. Dia de la independencia. It’s probably going to be a shitshow. But then again, I thought cinco de mayo was going to be a shitshow here, and it wasn’t. I’ll go with the flow, and stay up late if it’s loud.

My one big win today was getting a rewards payout at Linode. My VPS bill is paid for two additional months.

If you are interested in playing with a VPS, I recommend Linode. Linode is a mature company that’s beaten most of the kinks out of their system. Their Arch Linux image works. The only downside is you can’t try them before you load up their system with at least $10 in credit, and you have to use a credit card to do it – no paypal or Bitcoin.

Anyway, if you feel VPS-y, I’ve added my Linode rewards link to the bottom of the page. If you click the link and sign up, you’ll get to fly a fancy VPS through cyberspace for $10 a month, and I’ll get a dopamine hit from knowing my VPS bill is even more covered onward into the future.

One reader suggested I try out qmail instead of Postfix, so perhaps I’ll fire up a testing Arch Linux VPS and see if I can wrangle qmail into sending and receiving email for me.

I should write more words, but none are coming to me.

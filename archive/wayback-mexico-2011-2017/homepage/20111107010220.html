Who I am: I’m a digital evolutionist. The digital world is constantly changing, this is why I write a free daily letter and blog about experiments in the digital world. I write a bi-weekly deeper, intimate, hands-on letter about evolving your digital work.

What I’m into: I’m curious about the ways we make money online by creating work in the evolving digital space. The way we publish is evolving rapidly — we’re still reading, but in very different places. The destiny of the digital word is uncertain, but I’m determined to find out what that future is. I experiment and report back on what’s working. If it’s not? I untether.

Why I do the work: I believe in taking care of our bodies in the digital age. This is why I practice yoga (I’m a 200 hour certified yoga teacher), and I eat as well as I can (raw/vegetarian as much as possible.) I live with only a few Things.

Who reads me? Writers, publishers, creatives, artists, yogis, location independent workers, traveling adventurers, and those who make a living from publishing online.

Featured: My work has been featured on The CBS Evening News with Katie Couric, The Guardian, and The Huffington Post. I’ve spoken about digital publishing at Stanford University.

Adoption curve

Where are you on the adoption curve? Do you adopt early or late?

If you had the first iPhone or got invited to Gmail in the first week, then you’re a genius. But if you’re still on Twitter or Instagram right now, then are you surrounded by shitheads?

The first couple of people who watched a television were geniuses. But now?

Whether you agree or not, there are some people who are very rich now because they adopted Bitcoin in the first few years.

Does everyone you see at the cafe with a Macbook appear to be a shithead. Maybe it’s because they are.

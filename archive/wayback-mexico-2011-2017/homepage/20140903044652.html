How much does your data weigh (on your soul)?

Imagine all of your data is a physical thing. There’s an earthquake, or a fire. You have to go. All of it needs to go into your backpack.

How much does your data weigh?

All of your emails.

All of your photos.

All of your music.

All of those television shows you watched once and then saved to your hard drive.

All of your writing.

What else do you have? Go ahead and write it down somewhere.

How much does all of this weigh?

Well, it doesn’t weigh anything. That’s what we’ve been told. Data storage is almost infinite. You can go to a computer store and buy a 1 terabyte hard drive, and then keep throwing shit onto it until you have no shit to left to throw.

But how much does this data weigh? What is the weight of your data?

There are a few ways you can find out. Next time you attempt to find that document you threw onto your hard drive “somewhere”, time yourself. How much time did you lose trying to find it? The next time you spend an hour flipping through photos of long lost friends, time yourself. How much time did you lose drenched in nostalgia?

Everyone creates data. Few people delete it. You have to have huevos to delete your shit.

What would happen if you lost everything? You never backed up that hard drive, and then your drive crashes. Are you devastated?

Are you keeping redundant backups? I doubt it.

Are you storing all of your shit in with a false sense of security in “the cloud”. The bonus here, of course, is anyone at the NSA can also wax nostalgic about your college nudie pics.

Here’s an idea. Go get yourself a 4 gigabyte USB stick. They’re less than $95 pesos at Sanborns in Mexico City right now. Why don’t you buy two, while you’re at it.

Now see how much of your data can fit on that stick. This forces you to prioritize. This forces you to decide what deserves to survive, and what doesn’t.

See how much of your digital life you can fit into the small confines of 4 gigabytes.

Now delete the rest. One by one, if you have to. Or all at once, if you want to feel the adrenaline jolt that comes from freeing yourself all at once.

Your data did weigh something, didn’t it?

Well, now all of that weight is gone.

If you weren’t still made of meat and bones, you’d be flying.

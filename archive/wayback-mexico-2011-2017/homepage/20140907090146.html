Terminalism

How to live in a dying world.

What matters most is how you react when shit hits the fan.

And every moment that leads up to that moment is training for the moment when it happens.

How shit hits the fan is unclear. It’s impossible to predict the future.

I can tell the alarm bells are ringing in your head. If you can’t hear them, you’ve either found a way to check out, or you’re not listening hard enough.

These days it’s not uncommon to run into people who believe the currency is going to hyperinflate, or WWIII will start somewhere in Europe. Perhaps you have your own beliefs in the inevitable.

Whatever the inevitable will be, it’s probably going to be bad.

Terminalism prepares you for the moment when ‘it could be worse’ turns into ‘it’s worse.’

But I didn’t.

September 18, 2014

A memorial cigarette, for the programmer who jumped off the Golden Gate bridge on January 1st 2014.

Why? How the fuck do you answer that.

Because no one was fucking honest. No one. The whole time.

Now is not a time for dreamers. It’s not a time for idealists. It’s not a time for optimism. But most of all it’s not a time for lies and bullshit.

Maybe if everyone had been straight the whole thing wouldn’t have fallen apart. Or maybe it would have anyway.

Let’s put the world back together with what is up.

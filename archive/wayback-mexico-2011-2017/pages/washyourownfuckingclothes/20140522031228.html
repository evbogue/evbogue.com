This summer, I have no idea what I'm doing

One thing per day. If you miss it, contact me and I will send the one from yesterday.

2014-05-21

Miles asks, what is my end game with the new website approach?

Here’s what I noticed.

  1. I wanted to go back and edit all pieces on my website for accuracy
  2. This overwhelmed me, and made me feel terrible
  3. I have three stable products that have been edited multiple times
  4. The posts I wrote helped create those products
  5. But now what?
  6. So I deleted all of the posts

Unlike almost everyone else out there on the Internet.

I have no idea what I’m doing. I know I’ve built my perfect machine. I designed a beautiful responsible framework that worked for me. I live in a chaotic capitalist country with a view of the mountains when the smog clears.

But beyond that? I need to delete as much as possible to find what the throughline will be for the future.

We’re on the other side of social media and the Internet is a ghost town. It’s time to (re)build.

From 2013 to 2014 we watched as the social networking mirage of cloud-based nonsense became the myth we all wished it wasn’t. There are only a few things still standing in on the distributed web.

Today it’s more true than ever that getting a website up is the most important action you can take.

More important, you need to build it yourself.

It doesn’t need to be glamorous. In fact, it’s better if it’s not. There’s no need to learn backbone.js or Ruby on fucking Rails to get your website out into the wild. We’re all minimalists here, so we want to have as little ambition as possible. Once you get a minimum viable website up, you can customize and tweak all you want. But until you have a minimum viable website, you have nothing. So let’s start with nothing, and work towards something.

During the month of October, you’re going to build out that first minimum viable website. You own it. You control it. You know how to manage it. You break it, you fix it.

FAQ

I’ll update this over the next few days with your questions… Got a question?

Why do I need a website?

Websites are world-wide telepathy. What program do you use most on your computer? A web browser. As the age of social networking dies, we’re left with the world wide web, and the world wide web doesn’t do much good for you if your only web presence is a social profile behind a wall on a dying social network. What you do with your website is up to you, the important thing is to get one up.

But I already have a website! Someone built it for me 10 years ago

Delete it. Your website is old, boring, and no one cares. The point of MVW is to start from scratch and get a modern website out the door using the technologies people use to create websites right now in 2014.

Why do people come to evbogue.com, but not my website?

Because they went to your website once upon a time and then they hit the back button. Why? Because they couldn’t bring themselves to care about your website. Maybe you never updated it, or had to pay someone else to update it. Maybe you had good intentions, but kept on getting dragged back into the blue feces box. The best way to get people to start caring about your website again is to scrap it. Right now. Let’s start over with a new one.

But I want to reach more people…

The best way to reach more people is to have a minimum viable website you know how to update using your own two hands and a keyboard.

What’s the difference between DYW and MVW?

Design focues on teaching you how to code in HTML5 and CSS3. I wrote DYW because I needed a resource to recommend to people learning HTML5/CSS3 from scratch, and I couldn’t find a decent resource on the Internet. So I wrote one. MVW focues on building your website and the logistics of deploying it to the public web with daily actions.
